# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Achilles to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Give and receive feedback gracefully; review code, not the author.
- Keep discussion on topic and useful. Security-sensitive details go through the private
  channel in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not public threads.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal or political attacks, trolling, publishing others'
private information, and sustained disruption of discussion are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and contributions that
do not follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for
behavior they judge inappropriate. To report a problem, contact the maintainer through
their GitHub profile ([@Warzuponus](https://github.com/Warzuponus)) or, for anything you
would rather not say publicly, a private security advisory on this repository. Reports
are handled confidentially.
